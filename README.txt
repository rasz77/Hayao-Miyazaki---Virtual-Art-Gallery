THE WORLDS OF MIYAZAKI — SIMPLE CLASS PROJECT

1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Put these real, appropriately credited image files into the images folder:
   hayao-miyazaki.jpg
   chihiro.jpg
   totoro.jpg
   howl.jpg
   mononoke.jpg
   kiki.jpg
3. Find official film stills via https://www.ghibli.jp/works/ and each film's link on the site.
4. For Miyazaki's portrait, choose a photo with a suitable license and add photographer, source and license to the caption and references.
5. Video links currently open searches for official interviews/trailers; replace them with specific verified official video URLs when chosen.
6. Keep the source credits. Classroom use does not automatically mean images and videos are copyright-free.

The HTML/CSS/JS works without external libraries. Images are NOT bundled; they need to be sourced and credited.
